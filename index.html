About RISE 2026

RISE (Research, Innovation, Society & Entrepreneurship) is IIIT-Bangalore's flagship annual open house event that brings together researchers, industry leaders, students, and innovators. Explore cutting-edge research across multiple domains, interact with leading researchers, and discover opportunities for collaboration and growth.

Keynote Speaker

Mr. Anand Rangarajan

Vice President, Google Deepmind

Anand is a VP, Engineering in Google DeepMind. He joined Google in California in 2008 and worked in Gmail Ads and YouTube in Mountain View before moving to Bangalore in 2013. He has since led teams in Enterprise, Search and Google Pay in India. He is excited about solving challenging AI problems at scale. In prior life, he enjoyed designing, coding, and managing teams working in various aspects of Computer Networking. He fondly remembers the day when his startup Sahasra Networks was acquired by Cypress Semiconductor. He treasures the company of his B.Tech classmates from IIT Madras and Masters friends from Stanford University. He enjoys playing chess, running, word puzzles, sanskrit and listening to indian classical music. His cofounder and he enjoy nurturing their 2 startups aged 17 and 14.
